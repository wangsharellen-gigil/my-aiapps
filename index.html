The Duty to Mitigate

减损义务：合同违约后的合理应对

When a party breaches a contract, the injured party is generally expected to take reasonable steps to mitigate its losses. This principle does not require the claimant to take extraordinary measures; it asks only that the claimant act as a reasonable person would under similar circumstances.

For example, if a supplier fails to deliver essential materials, a business may be expected to seek a substitute supplier rather than allow production to stop indefinitely. The costs of obtaining replacement goods may then be recoverable as damages, provided that those costs are reasonable and sufficiently connected to the breach.

✳ 长难句拆解

Although the claimant is not required to incur disproportionate expense, any avoidable loss that results from a failure to take reasonable steps may be excluded from the damages awarded.

结构：Although 让步状语从句 + 主句；主句中 that results... 修饰 loss，而 that results... 的主语是 loss。

The burden of proving a failure to mitigate normally rests on the party in breach. A court will assess the claimant's conduct in context, rather than with the benefit of hindsight. The question is whether the steps taken were reasonable at the time they were taken.

The Parol Evidence Rule

口头证据规则：书面合同与先前协商

The parol evidence rule governs the use of evidence of prior or contemporaneous agreements when the parties have reduced their agreement to a final written contract. In many common-law jurisdictions, a court may limit such evidence if the writing was intended to be the complete and final expression of the parties' agreement.

The rule is not an absolute ban on all outside evidence. Evidence may still be admitted to explain an ambiguous term, establish that a contract was induced by fraud, or show that the writing was subject to a condition that never occurred. The precise approach varies by jurisdiction.

✳ 长难句拆解

Where a written agreement is found to be a complete integration, prior statements that contradict its terms will generally be excluded, even if a party later claims that those statements reflected the parties' true intentions.

结构：Where 引导条件状语从句；主句为被动语态 will be excluded，that contradict its terms 修饰 statements。

Courts therefore examine both the language of the document and the circumstances surrounding its formation. The rule seeks to promote certainty in written agreements while leaving room for evidence relevant to the validity or interpretation of a contract.
